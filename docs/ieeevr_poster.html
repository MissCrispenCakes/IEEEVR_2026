"What changes when an AI partner joins movement — not as a text box, but as a felt presence?"

The Problem

XR interaction is embodied — you lean, reach, hesitate, feel a pull.

Generative AI pulls creative making toward text: stop moving, start explaining. That interruption breaks the kinaesthetic logic of immersive creation.

Can an AI partner stay legible and negotiable when the interface is the body?

Design Lenses
  • ProTactile — tactile grounding, back-channeling, repair in real time
  • Interspecies coordination — orientation and invitation, not commands
  • Consentful interaction* — ongoing, renegotiable consent
